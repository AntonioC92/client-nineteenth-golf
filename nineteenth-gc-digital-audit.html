01

Executive summary

This audit sets the baseline for Nineteenth Golf Club's digital presence before we switch on paid media. The site itself looks great, clean, modern, and working well on mobile.

The gap is underneath the surface, where the measurement and tracking layer is effectively unbuilt. There's no Google Analytics, no Google Tag Manager, no Google Ads conversion tag, no LocalBusiness schema, no verified Meta Pixel on the main domain, and no pixel instrumentation on the third-party booking platform that handles every paying customer.

In practical terms, every ad dollar spent today lands in a blind funnel. We can't see which campaign, creative, or keyword drove a booking. Getting the tracking and schema layer sorted is the highest-leverage thing we can do in Month 1, well ahead of any creative or bidding work.

Beyond tracking, there are three UX issues worth fixing quickly: pricing isn't visible on the public site, the booking flow hands off to an external domain with no branded return, and there's no post-booking email sequence to bring people back. All three are solvable within the first 30 days.

Top 5 priorities

  • 1Install GA4 + Google Tag Manager on the Framer site. Everything else depends on this.
  • 2Install and verify Meta Pixel on nineteenthgc.com and fire a Lead event on Book Now clicks.
  • 3Solve cross-domain booking tracking via a redirect or thank-you page, or Conversions API. Booking completions must be attributable.
  • 4Add LocalBusiness JSON-LD schema + robots.txt/sitemap.xml to unlock local SEO and Google Business Profile visibility.
  • 5Surface pricing on site and add testimonials and reviews. These are the two biggest friction points in the conversion journey.
02

Website (nineteenthgc.com)

Live Good foundation · Weak measurement layer
  • Single-page structure with /pricing and /gallery subpages, all carrying a clean and modern design.
  • The viewport tag is present with responsive breakpoints at 810px and 1200px, and the mobile nav works as expected.
  • The hero has a video background, the headline "Your game. Your time.", a clear "Book your bay now" CTA, and the TRIAL25 offer code surfaced upfront.
  • The free session duration isn't specified anywhere in the hero, so visitors have no way of knowing if it's 30 minutes or an hour.
  • All lead capture is offsite, with no email capture, no enquiry form, and no newsletter sign-up anywhere on the site.
  • The stylesheet is inline rather than an external file, which has a minor effect on caching and load performance.
  • The hero image is 4608x2976 with srcset for responsive handling, and the video uses preload="auto", so LCP on slow connections is worth watching.
SeverityIssueRecommendation
High Pricing is not visible on the homepage. Users must click through to /pricing or enter the booking flow to see cost. Add a three-tier price summary block on the homepage (single session, 10-pack, membership) directly under the hero.
High No trust signals on the homepage. No reviews, testimonials, or star ratings. Embed a Google review widget or a 3-quote testimonial band above the footer, linked to the full Google Business Profile.
Medium "Free session" offer is ambiguous in duration. Reword the hero CTA subhead: "Your first 30-minute session free with code TRIAL25".
Medium No on-site lead capture. Visitors who are not ready to book have no way to stay in touch. Add an email capture unit ("Get 10% off your first booking") routing to Mailchimp, Klaviyo, or similar.
Low Inline CSS reduces browser caching efficiency. Externalise global stylesheet once Framer permits. Non-urgent.
03

Meta Pixel

Not installed Not verified on nineteenthgc.com · Definitely missing on the booking platform
  • No fbq() initialisation block, no connect.facebook.net/en_US/fbevents.js script, and no 16-digit Pixel ID found in the homepage HTML.
  • There's a Facebook profile link in the footer, but that's a page link, not a pixel.
  • The entire booking flow (date, time, payment, confirmation) happens on yourgolfbooking.com, which is a completely separate domain. Nothing the main site pixel sees extends to what happens over there.
  • The agreed workaround is a redirect landing page so pixel events can fire on return. That page hasn't been built yet.
  • The practical result today is that every paid booking coming via Meta looks like a non-conversion. CPR and ROAS are completely unreadable.
SeverityIssueRecommendation
Critical No verified Meta Pixel on the main domain. Create Pixel in Meta Business Suite, install base code in Framer head, verify with Meta Pixel Helper. Fire PageView site-wide.
Critical Zero conversion visibility for bookings due to cross-domain break. Implement the redirect: YGB confirmation page sends the visitor to nineteenthgc.com/thank-you?booking=1. Fire Purchase and Lead events on that page.
High No Conversions API fallback. iOS 17, ITP, and ad-blockers will suppress a meaningful percentage of browser pixel fires. Stand up CAPI via Zapier, Stape.io, or direct webhook from YGB. Dedupe with the browser event via event_id.
High No Lead event on Book Now clicks. Add fbq('track','Lead') on the click handler for every Book Now CTA. Used as a soft-conversion for mid-funnel campaigns.
Medium No custom audiences can be built yet. Once pixel is live, create audiences for all visitors (180d), pricing-page viewers, and Book Now clickers without purchase, ready for retargeting.
04

Google Tags (GA4, GTM, Google Ads)

Not installed Green-field install required across all Google products
  • No GTM container, no GA4 measurement ID, no Google Ads conversion tag, and no legacy UA- tag found anywhere on the site.
  • This is a completely fresh install across all Google products, which is actually the cleanest scenario to work with.
  • The result is zero visibility into traffic sources, landing page performance, device mix, bounce rate, scroll depth, or campaign attribution.
SeverityIssueRecommendation
Critical No GA4 property running. Create GA4 property, install via GTM (not direct gtag), wire core events: page_view, book_now_click, external_click to YGB, phone_call, email_click.
Critical No Google Tag Manager container. Create a GTM container. Add the GTM snippet to Framer site head and body. Manage Pixel, GA4, and Ads tags through GTM. Hard-coding tags directly should be avoided from this point forward.
Critical No Google Ads conversion tracking. Create Google Ads account or link existing. Generate a Purchase conversion action. Fire it from the same /thank-you page used for the Pixel.
High No UTM taxonomy in use. Define standard UTM conventions (source, medium, campaign, content) for every paid link and social bio link. Document in a shared sheet.
Medium No Search Console property. Verify nineteenthgc.com in Google Search Console via GTM or DNS. Connect to GA4 for query data.
05

SEO (on-page, technical, local)

Partial Basics present · Local SEO layer missing
  • The title tag reads "Nineteenth Golf Club", which is brand name only with no keywords, leaving real estate on the table.
  • The meta description is well-written and around 150 characters, which is a good length.
  • The canonical tag (https://nineteenthgc.com/) is present and correct, and OpenGraph and Twitter card tags are both in place.
  • robots.txt and sitemap.xml couldn't be retrieved on the audit date, so they're either absent or not published through Framer's default setup.
  • No Schema.org JSON-LD was detected anywhere on the site, meaning no LocalBusiness, GolfCourse, or Organization markup, which is the single biggest local SEO gap.
  • NAP (Name, Address, Phone) is consistent across the site and external listings: Unit 6/90-94 Carnarvon Street, Silverwater NSW 2128, +61 2 7245 3898, admin@nineteenthgc.com.
  • The Google Business Profile appears to exist (the venue shows in TrustIndex and ClassPass) but has low visibility and isn't the dominant result for brand searches.
  • With just three indexable pages (/, /pricing, /gallery), there's very little surface area for the site to compete on non-brand keywords.
SeverityIssueRecommendation
Critical No LocalBusiness or SportsActivityLocation JSON-LD schema. Add JSON-LD block with name, address, geo, openingHours (24/7), telephone, url, priceRange, image, sameAs (IG/FB URLs). This is the lever for Local Pack and knowledge panel.
High robots.txt and sitemap.xml not publicly retrievable. Enable Framer's SEO settings to publish sitemap.xml. Add robots.txt pointing to it. Submit sitemap in Google Search Console.
High Title tag is brand-only and leaves keyword real estate on the table. Rewrite to: "Nineteenth Golf Club | 24/7 Golf Simulator Sydney · Silverwater" (under 60 characters).
High Google Business Profile is under-optimised. Claim or verify GBP. Add 15+ photos (bays, Trackman screens, lounge, exterior). Fill all categories, 24/7 hours, products, posts, Q&A. Launch a review-request email flow.
Medium Only 2-3 indexable pages. No content to rank for long-tail queries. Build a /blog or /journal section targeting: "golf simulator sydney", "indoor golf silverwater", "trackman near me", "after-hours golf practice sydney".
Medium No BreadcrumbList or FAQPage schema. Add FAQPage schema to /pricing answering the top 6 questions: cost, what's included, cancellation, parking, age, how booking works.
Low No H1/H2 keyword discipline. Headings are brand and slogan-led. Keep the creative headline visually, but ensure the DOM H1 contains the category term: "24/7 Golf Simulator in Silverwater, Sydney".
06

Booking system

Functional External platform · Significant tracking gaps
  • Third-party platform: Your Golf Booking (YGB) at yourgolfbooking.com/venues/nineteenth-gc/booking.
  • Every Book Now CTA takes the visitor off nineteenthgc.com and onto YGB, where date, bay, contact details, and payment are all captured.
  • Apple Pay, Google Pay, and card payments appear to be supported, making the payment experience reasonably low-friction.
  • Memberships and bay selection are available as separate YGB URLs, and the promo code field accepts TRIAL25.
  • There's no mechanism in place to pass a confirmed booking event back to nineteenthgc.com for Pixel, GA4, or Ads attribution.
  • The customer journey ends on a third-party domain with no branded thank-you page, which hurts both the brand experience and measurement.
SeverityIssueRecommendation
Critical Bookings complete on an external domain with no event pass-back. Set up the redirect so the YGB confirmation page sends visitors to nineteenthgc.com/thank-you. Fire Pixel Purchase, GA4 purchase, and Google Ads conversion on that page.
High No server-side fallback. Browser pixel will miss a portion of conversions. Add Conversions API via webhook from YGB or a scheduled Zap matching email to event. Use event_id dedup between browser and server.
High Pricing is only visible inside the booking flow. Mirror the YGB pricing onto /pricing and the homepage. Remove the need to enter the booking flow just to learn the cost.
Medium Domain switch weakens trust and branding. Where YGB permits, use a custom domain (book.nineteenthgc.com) so the URL stays on-brand even if hosting is external.
Medium Guest-checkout behaviour unclear. Possible forced account creation. Confirm guest checkout is enabled in YGB settings. If not, enable or negotiate with YGB.
Medium No visible cancellation, refund, ID, or age policy pre-booking. Add a short "Good to know" block on /pricing covering these points before the user commits.

Conversion journey

Functional but fragmented Two hand-off points break tracking
Steps 1-2: Awareness and consideration

The hero does its job. "Your game. Your time." with 24/7 positioning and the TRIAL25 offer is a strong opening.

Pricing and gallery pages exist, but pricing isn't surfaced on the homepage, and there are no reviews or testimonials anywhere on site.

Steps 3-4: Decision and action

"Book your bay now" sends the visitor to yourgolfbooking.com. That's where Pixel and GA4 lose them.

Bay selection, time, details, and payment all happen on YGB. No data flows back to nineteenthgc.com.

Step 5: Post-booking

YGB sends a confirmation email. Beyond that, there's nothing. No branded follow-up, no review request, no prompt to book again, no membership nudge.

Trust signals on-site

Address, phone, map link, and an Instagram embed are all present.

What's missing: a star rating or review count, testimonials, team profiles, and any press or partner logos. A Trackman partner badge would be a quick, high-credibility addition.

SeverityIssueRecommendation
Critical Cross-domain attribution is broken at two hand-offs. Fix via redirect and /thank-you page (see sections 3 and 6). Until fixed, all paid-media reporting is unreliable.
High No post-booking lifecycle email sequence. Build a 4-email flow: D+1 welcome and pre-session tips, D+3 review request to Google, D+7 "book your next bay", D+14 membership upsell. Use Mailchimp, Klaviyo, or Brevo.
High Pricing friction before commitment. Surface pricing on the homepage (addressed in sections 2 and 5).
Medium No visible social proof on-site. Pull 3-5 Google reviews (JSON-LD and widget) into the homepage above the footer.
Medium No SMS reminder confirmed. Confirm YGB SMS reminders are on. This reduces no-show rate.
Low No branded thank-you moment. The thank-you page doubles as an attribution beacon and brand touchpoint. Design it like a mini-reward: next steps, WiFi, parking, what to bring.

30 / 60 / 90-day roadmap

The sequencing below is designed so that paid media in Month 2 runs against a clean, measurable funnel. Tracking infrastructure goes in first. Campaigns only go live once attribution is confirmed and working.

Days 0-30: Foundation Month 1
  • Install GTM. Install GA4 through GTM. Verify core events.
  • Install and verify Meta Pixel site-wide. Add Lead event to Book Now clicks.
  • Build the redirect /thank-you page. Wire Purchase events for Pixel, GA4, and Google Ads.
  • Add LocalBusiness JSON-LD schema. Publish robots.txt and sitemap.xml. Submit to Search Console.
  • Claim and optimise Google Business Profile: photos, hours, products, posts, review flow.
  • Add pricing block and 3-quote testimonial band to the homepage.
  • Rewrite title tag and hero subhead to specify the 30-minute trial.
  • Confirm Business Manager links: Page, Pixel, Ads, Instagram, and billing under one asset group.
Days 31-60: Activate Month 2
  • Stand up Meta Conversions API via YGB webhook or Zapier. Dedupe with browser pixel.
  • Launch 3-tier Meta funnel: awareness, consideration, conversion.
  • Launch Google Search campaign on branded and high-intent non-brand keywords.
  • Build and activate post-booking email sequence: D+1, D+3, D+7, D+14.
  • Lock content calendar: IG 2 Reels, 2 statics, 1 carousel, daily stories. FB 2-3 posts per week.
  • Launch review-request flow to existing bookers. Target 25+ Google reviews by Day 60.
Days 61-90: Optimise Month 3
  • First CPR, CAC, and ROAS review. Kill bottom-quartile creative. Scale winners.
  • Launch retargeting audiences: all visitors, pricing viewers, Book Now abandoners.
  • Add 4 blog posts targeting long-tail local keywords.
  • Add FAQPage schema to /pricing.
  • Launch #MyNineteenthGC UGC loop and one micro-influencer collaboration.
  • Membership upsell campaign to second-time bookers.
