04

Actions required before launch

Four items are blocking the campaign from going live. Three require action from the client or site builder. One will be completed by Streetwise once the GA4 link is approved. Each item is assigned to a specific owner below.

Client - Account Owner

Approve the GA4 → Google Ads link

In Google Ads, go to Tools → Data Manager → Received tab. There is one pending request from GA4, dated 12 Jun 2026. Click Review and approve it. This requires Admin-level access to account 638-932-0476. Once approved, the GA4 purchase event will flow into Google Ads.

Streetwise - Once Link is Approved

Create the conversion action in Google Ads

After the link is active, Streetwise will go to Goals → Conversions → Create → Import from Google Analytics 4 and select the purchase event. Conversion action settings: Name: GA4 – Booking Confirmed · Category: Purchase · Value: A$35 · Count: Once.

Sean - Framer Site

Create the /thank-you page on the website

A page needs to exist at nineteenthgc.com/thank-you. Content can be simple: "Thanks for your booking, we'll see you on the course." The GTM conversion tag is already configured to fire when any visitor lands on this URL.

Sean - YGB Admin

Set post-booking redirect to /thank-you

After a completed booking in YGB (Your Golf Booking), the venue must redirect the customer to nineteenthgc.com/thank-you. Sean needs to confirm or set this redirect in the YGB venue admin settings.

05

Pre-launch checklist

The campaign will be unpaused only after all five steps are confirmed. Steps 1 to 4 depend on the actions above. Step 5 is the final verification Streetwise will run before switching the campaign live.

  • 1
    GA4 → Google Ads link approved

    Client approves in Google Ads → Data Manager → Received. Streetwise confirms link shows "Active" status.

  • 2
    Google Ads conversion action created and set to recording

    Streetwise creates the import from GA4 → purchase event. Conversion action must show "Recording conversions" in the Goals tab before proceeding.

  • 3
    /thank-you page live on website

    Sean publishes the confirmation page at nineteenthgc.com/thank-you. Page must load successfully in a browser before proceeding.

  • 4
    YGB post-booking redirect confirmed

    Sean confirms (or sets) the post-booking redirect in YGB venue settings to point to nineteenthgc.com/thank-you.

  • 5
    End-to-end tracking test passes

    Visit /thank-you → GTM Preview confirms purchase tag fires → GA4 DebugView shows purchase event → Google Ads conversion status shows "Recording conversions". Campaign 23929932123 is then unpaused and goes live.
