What it measures
Every "Book Now" click that reaches the booking platform, the clearest available signal of intent
begin_checkout conversion strategy The begin_checkout event fires when a visitor clicks "Book Now" and is sent to the booking platform. The booking platform does not support post-purchase redirects, so tracking measures booking intent, the CTA click through to the booking platform, rather than completed bookings. This is a known constraint of the platform and the conversion action is set up accordingly.
