How do you stack up against the pros? 👀⛳ Last week we shared the PGA Tour TrackMan averages. This week we're starting to unpack what those numbers actually mean for your own swing. Part 1 · Club Path 🏌️ 📊 What it is: the horizontal direction the club head is travelling at impact, measured in degrees off the target line ➡️ Positive numbers mean your path is heading right of target, which is in-to-out for a right-hander ⬅️ Negative numbers mean your path is heading left of target, which is out-to-in for a right-hander 🎯 Combined with the angle of the club face at impact, club path is what shapes the curve of every shot A few quick scenarios for a right-hander: ↩️ Face closed to the path produces a draw, with the ball curving back to the left ↪️ Face open to the path produces a fade, with the ball curving out to the right ⬆️ Face matching the path produces a straight ball, which is rarer than people think Rule of thumb worth saving: Face wins the start. Path wins the curve. The pros build their entire game off these numbers. Why wouldn't you? 👀 Come see your own readout on any bay at any hour.
