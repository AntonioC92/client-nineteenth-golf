01

What I built

This is the first paid Meta campaign for Nineteenth. It goes live the day after Sean's foundation week ships and the Pixel is verified.

I kept the brief deliberately small. One campaign, one ad set, one offer ('Trial25' = first 30 minutes free), and two creative formats running side by side. That way we can read which format the Sydney golf audience actually responds to before scaling spend in June.

Both ads use existing facility footage and photos. Nothing new was shot for round one.

Optimisation rules

RuleTrigger / action
Don't touch · Days 1 to 7No edits, no budget changes, no creative swaps. Meta's learning resets every time the ad set is meaningfully edited.
Day 10 checkpointIf fewer than 5 Purchase events have logged, switch the optimisation event to Lead (Book-Now click). Keep Purchase as the headline KPI for reporting.
Kill rule · creativeIf after Day 14 one ad's cost-per-Purchase is > 2.5× the other, with at least 3 Purchases on the winner, I pause the loser.
Kill rule · placementI pause Audience Network if it's delivering > 15% of impressions but < 5% of Purchases.
Scale ruleIf by Day 21 cost-per-Purchase is < $40 and I've logged ≥ 10 bookings, I raise the daily budget to $35 for the final 9 days.
Frequency safetyIf frequency crosses 3.0 by Day 21, I expand the radius to 35 km or widen the age band to 22 to 60. Audience adjustments fix frequency fatigue at this volume; the creative isn't the issue yet.
