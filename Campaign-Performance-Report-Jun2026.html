📊 Understanding the Conversion Number (55 from 34 clicks)

The conversion count will always look higher than clicks — and that's expected. Here's why:

Conversions track a "Begin Checkout" event — this fires every time someone clicks the booking button on the Nineteenth Golf website to open the YGB booking flow. A single visitor can click that button multiple times in one session (e.g. browsing different session times), and each click registers as a separate conversion.

This is intentional. The YGB booking platform doesn't expose the final "thank you / booking confirmed" page to tracking scripts, so we use the booking button click as the closest available signal of high purchase intent. A visitor reaching this step is actively trying to book a session — not just browsing.

💡  Think of it as: 34 people clicked the ad → 55 booking-intent interactions triggered → A$1.18 cost per high-intent action
