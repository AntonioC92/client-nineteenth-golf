Phase 1 baseline — 28 days

Phase 1 built the tracking foundation and proved the funnel works. This plan covers the first 30 days of Phase 2: closing out the remaining data layer, launching new campaigns, and scaling budget as creative refreshes. Direction C (Google Search and email flow) begins in Month 2 once this foundation is set.

Four-Week Delivery Plan
Week 1 Days 1 to 7

Close out the tracking layer

Before scaling spend, we need the data layer complete so every conversion has a second source of truth.

  • Build the on-brand /thank-you confirmation page on Framer, with the Christina Kang free swing assessment as a post-booking nudge
  • Verify GA4 end-to-end through Google Tag Manager
  • Tidy the auto-events feed and standardise the conversion event flow
  • Ship the Meta attributed customers explainer document
  • Daily campaign management and monitoring
Week 2 Days 8 to 14

New campaigns live

Trial25 keeps running. Week 2 launches two new ad sets alongside it.

  • Build and launch the memberships and bundles campaign in Meta Ads Manager
  • Stand up three retargeting audiences: ad engagers who did not visit, visitors who did not click Book Now, and clickers who did not book
  • Use foundation memberships and the Christina Kang free swing assessment as the primary offer hooks
  • Weekly report
Week 3 Days 15 to 21

Scale budget and refresh creative

Frequency is at 3.26. New creative comes before the budget lift.

  • Refresh the creative pool with new video variants built for mobile (98% of conversions come through the Facebook and Instagram apps)
  • Increase total monthly budget to AUD $1,200 to $1,500 as creative goes live
  • Build a Lookalike Audience from the 121 confirmed purchasers in the Meta Pixel
  • Weekly report
Week 4 Days 22 to 30

Consolidate and prepare Month 2

Review what the data is saying and lay the groundwork for Google Search.

  • Publish the first monthly local SEO content piece
  • Provide Google Reviews copy support (NGC leads in-person, email and SMS rollout)
  • Begin Google Search keyword research and budget scoping for Direction C
  • Set up the monthly scorecard: members, booked hours, ad spend, cost per booking
  • Month 2 kickoff plan
Ongoing throughout
  • Daily campaign monitoring and optimisation
  • Weekly performance report every Friday: spend, reach, cost per booking, key observations
